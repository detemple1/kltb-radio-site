The familiar voices

Meet the people of KLTB.

Every great station has voices that feel like old friends. Get to know the people keeping Tahoe company, from first light through the quietest hours.

Weekdays · 6–9 AM & 9 PM–Midnight

Jake Ellis

The Breakfast Club / Whispers After Dark

Start the morning with Tahoe's familiar voice, then return after dark for music, conversation, and stories that take on a different meaning at night.

Meet Jake

Known as the Voice of the Lake, Jake prefers the microphone to the spotlight. His audience knows his sound; the man behind it remains something of a mystery.

Daily · 9 AM–3 PM

Midday Marcus

Sounds at Work

An easygoing soundtrack for the workday, blending thoughtful music selections with a little perspective, warm conversation, and a welcome break from the afternoon rush.

Meet Marcus

The station's resident professor and oldest personality, Marcus is affectionately known as the old man on the hill. His wit is gentle, his musical taste broad, and his curiosity endless.

Daily · 3–9 PM

Pete Svenson

Drive Time

From the afternoon commute into the evening, Pete brings upbeat music, quick humor, and the kind of energy that makes the long way home worthwhile.

Meet Pete

Early thirties, single, and happiest outdoors, Pete spends his free time hiking, kayaking, or playing beach volleyball. He brings that same easy confidence and enthusiasm to the air.

Daily · Midnight–6 AM

Overnight Otis

Overnights with Otis

When the lake settles down and the roads grow quiet, Otis keeps the lights on with great music, good company, and an infectious sense of humor.

Meet Otis

Otis is the friendly face behind the midnight microphone: a cheerful guy who, despite never being seen in anything but all black, always seems to find the silver lining in everything.

Weekdays · 6–9 AM & 3–6 PM

BethAnn Schrader

News · Weather · Traffic

From morning road conditions to the afternoon forecast, BethAnn keeps Tahoe informed with clear updates, local perspective, and a smile you can hear over the radio.

Meet BethAnn

A retired schoolteacher and proud soccer mom in her early fifties, BethAnn brings cheerful curls, colorful glasses, and a practical sense of humor to both daily shifts.

Station operations

Sameera “Sammi” Mazari

Office Manager & Traffic Coordinator

Keeping the station organized, advertisers on schedule, and KLTB running smoothly behind the scenes.

Meet Sammi

Sammi moved from London to Lake Tahoe ten years ago after falling in love with the area during a summer visit. A mother of four and a natural multitasker, she's the organizational heartbeat of KLTB and the person everyone counts on to get things done.
