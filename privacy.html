Privacy Policy

Adapted for KLTB · October 11, 2026

This website is operated by 82 Mercer, Inc., a California S-Corporation ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information we collect at kltbradio.com and its development website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

The short version: we collect an email address only if you hand us one, we use it to send you news about the station, its artists, programs, and projects, we don't sell it to anyone, and you can leave the mailing list at any time with one click.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address — when you subscribe to station updates, request a launch notification, or contact us.
  • Name — optional, when you provide it on a signup or contact form.
  • Message content — whatever you write to us through a contact form, once available.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our host records standard technical data when you visit: IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages you viewed. This is used to understand traffic and keep the site running. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

How we use your information

  • To send you the station updates you asked for — programming, artists, new music, releases, and occasional offers.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To understand which pages people read, so we can improve the site.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. The only parties who touch your data are service providers who process it on our behalf.

Email newsletter and consent

We only email people who have asked to hear from us. Subscribers join our list by submitting an opt-in form on this website or signing up through one of our station landing pages or launch-notification forms.

We do not purchase, rent, scrape, or otherwise acquire email addresses from third parties. If you're on our list, it's because you put yourself there.

Unsubscribing. Every marketing email we send contains an unsubscribe link in the footer. You can also email us directly to request removal.

Service providers

We use a small number of third-party services to run this site and its mailing list. Each is bound by its own privacy policy and processes data on our behalf:

  • Kit (ConvertKit) — email list management and delivery.
  • Netlify — website hosting.
  • Our email provider — handling email inquiries.

When streaming and contact services are activated, this section will be updated to identify the providers and relevant data handling.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies — the kind needed to make pages work correctly and to remember basic preferences. We do not currently run advertising trackers or third-party ad networks on this site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; basic site content will still work.

Links to other sites

Our pages may link to The Lake Effect Series, artists, and other outside sites. Once you click through, you're on their site and their privacy policy applies, not ours. We have no control over how they collect or use your data.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your information from our records.
  • Stop receiving email from us.

Email us and we'll take care of it. We won't discriminate against you for making a request.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, where applicable, you may have rights to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion, and to opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information.

EU and UK residents

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, our legal basis for sending you marketing email is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. You may also have rights to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

How long we keep your information

We keep subscriber information for as long as you remain on the list. If you unsubscribe, we retain a minimal record of your email address to avoid accidentally emailing you again. Contact-form messages, once that service is launched, will be kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us their information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Our site is served over HTTPS, and our service providers maintain safeguards. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting how we use your information will be announced to subscribers by email.
