Privacy Policy

Effective July 17, 2026 · Last updated July 17, 2026

This website is operated by David E. Temple through 82 Mercer, Inc., a California S-Corporation ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information we collect at kltbradio.com, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

The short version: we collect an email address only if you hand us one, we use it to send you KLTB station updates, we don't sell it to anyone, and you can leave at any time with one click.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Email address — when you sign up for station updates or contact us.
  • Name — when you use the contact form.
  • Message content — whatever you write to us through the contact form, including your reason for reaching out (song request, guest DJ inquiry, sponsorship, press, or general).

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our host records standard technical data when you visit: IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the pages you viewed. This is used to understand traffic and keep the site running. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

How we use your information

  • To send you the station updates you signed up for — new music, schedule changes, and word before anyone else.
  • To reply to messages you send us through the contact form.
  • To understand which pages people read, so we can improve the site.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Ever. The only parties who touch your data are the service providers listed below, who process it on our behalf.

Email updates and consent

We only email people who have asked to hear from us. Subscribers join our list by submitting the signup form on this website — that's the only way onto the list.

We do not purchase, rent, scrape, or otherwise acquire email addresses from third parties. If you're on our list, it's because you put yourself there.

Unsubscribing. Every email we send contains an unsubscribe link in the footer. One click removes you, permanently, with no follow-up and no questions. You can also email us directly and we'll remove you by hand.

Service providers

We use a small number of third-party services to run this site and its mailing list. Each is bound by its own privacy policy and processes data on our behalf:

  • Kit (ConvertKit) — email list management and delivery.
  • Netlify — website hosting and contact form handling.
  • iCloud Mail (Apple) — our email inbox.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies — the kind needed to make pages work correctly and to remember basic preferences. We do not run advertising trackers or third-party ad networks on this site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Links to other sites

This site links out to our social channels. Once you click through, you're on their platform and their privacy policy applies, not ours. We have no control over how they collect or use your data.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your information from our records.
  • Stop receiving email from us.

Email us and we'll take care of it. We won't discriminate against you for making a request.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion, and to opt out of its sale. We don't sell personal information, so there's nothing to opt out of — but the rights to know and to delete are yours, and the email above is how you exercise them.

EU and UK residents

Under the GDPR, our legal basis for sending you marketing email is your consent, which you gave when you subscribed and can withdraw at any time. You also have the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

How long we keep your information

We keep subscriber information for as long as you remain on the list. If you unsubscribe, we retain a minimal record of your email address for the sole purpose of making sure we don't accidentally email you again. Contact-form messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us their information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Our site is served over HTTPS, and our service providers maintain industry-standard safeguards. That said, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting how we use your information will be announced to subscribers by email.
